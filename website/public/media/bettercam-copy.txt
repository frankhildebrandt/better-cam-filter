PRODUKTBESCHREIBUNG (DE)
BetterCam ist eine native macOS-Webcam-App mit lokaler Hintergrundersetzung. Wähle deine Kamera, importiere einen Hintergrund und nutze das verarbeitete Bild über die virtuelle Kamera BetterCam in deinen Video-Apps. Mit Apple Vision, optionalen Core-ML-Modellen und Schutzbereichen gestaltest du dein Bild selbst. Die Ausgabe ist von HD bis 4K mit 15 bis 60 FPS konfigurierbar. Standard ist Full HD bei 30 FPS; die tatsächlich erreichbare Leistung hängt von Mac, Kamera und Modell ab. BetterCam steht als Quellcode zum Selbstbau unter der MIT-Lizenz bereit.

SHORT DESCRIPTION (EN)

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION (EN)
Choose a camera, import a background, and send your processed picture to video apps through the BetterCam virtual camera. Start with Apple Vision, download optional U²-Net models, or import your own Core ML model. Fixed keep regions let you preserve parts of the original picture. Output is configurable from HD to 4K at target frame rates between 15 and 60 FPS, with Full HD at 30 FPS as the default. Actual processing performance depends on your camera, Mac, and model. BetterCam is available as source code under the MIT license.

SOCIAL POST (EN)

FACTS AND EDITORIAL NOTES
- macOS 14+, Xcode with Swift 6, and XcodeGen for building.
- The virtual camera requires Apple signing and appropriate provisioning profiles. Developer ID distribution additionally requires notarization.
- No signed app download is offered on the website.
- Camera processing runs locally. Optional Codex background generation sends prompts, context, and reference images through Codex to OpenAI; live camera frames are not used for generation.
- Optional models have their own licenses. U²-Net/U²-NetP models are not bundled with the app.
- Target output formats are not performance guarantees.
- Keep regions are fixed image regions, not object tracking.
- The landing-page studio artwork is an illustration, not an app screenshot.
- No audio capture or video recording feature is included.
